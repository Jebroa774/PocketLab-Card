# Detailed feature design baseline

This document is the schematic and layout baseline for the non-power feature
blocks. It is deliberately more specific than the architecture notes, but it
is **not** a fabrication release: NFC tuning, controlled-impedance geometry,
antenna selection, ERC/DRC, and a first-article review remain mandatory.

The preferred assembly is top-side JLCPCB PCBA. Parts with hidden pads, LGA,
U.FL, and the microSD connector are factory-only. General passives remain 0805;
0603 is used only where RF performance or package geometry requires it.

## Board-wide interface rules

- `+3V3` is the logic rail. No ESP32, TCA9535, sensor, SD, or radio I/O is
  5 V tolerant.
- Fit one shared I2C pull-up pair only. Use 3.3 kOhm to `+3V3` as the default
  because the PN532 reference design uses 3.3 kOhm at 3.3 V. A 4.7 kOhm
  alternative is allowed only if measured SDA/SCL rise time remains below
  300 ns at 400 kHz with the assembled board and expansion header unloaded.
- The shared SPI bus is limited to 8 MHz in the first firmware because the E07
  module is specified to 10 MHz and the microSD branch increases bus loading.
  Put 33 Ohm source/branch damping footprints in the clock and driven branches.
- Every off-board digital net gets a series resistor footprint (default
  220 Ohm for low-speed GPIO) and an optional low-capacitance ESD array at the
  connector. Protection must be placed before the trace enters the board.
- All RF values below are initial values, not promises of a tuned antenna.
  C0G/NP0 capacitors and high-Q RF inductors are required.

## U2 PN532 NFC controller

The PN532 is retained because it matches the requested feature set and has a
known software ecosystem. NXP marks it **not recommended for new designs** and
recommends PN7160 for new products. Keep the NFC sheet sufficiently isolated
that a future controller revision is possible.

The PN532 I2C address is 0x24 in 7-bit notation (0x48 as the write address byte
used in older NXP documentation).

**not** supply inductors.

### Antenna and matching implementation

Use the symmetrical NXP starting topology on both TX paths:

- `TX1/TX2 -> L0 -> matching network -> loop`, with `L0 = 560 nH` and
  `C0 = 220 pF` on each side as the initial EMC-filter values.
- Provide two C0G 0603 tuning positions in parallel for each C1 and C2 branch.
  Expected C1/C2 values are in the 10 pF to 300 pF range, but must be calculated
  and then tuned from measurements.
- Provide one series damping position `RQ` per side, initially 0 Ohm plus DNP
  alternatives in the several-ohm range.
- Add two probe pads and removable 0 Ohm links between matching network and
  loop. They are needed to measure the bare loop and isolate the controller.

PCB-loop prototype geometry inside the reserved NFC area:

| Parameter | Initial value |
|---|---:|
| Outer size | 36.0 x 28.0 mm |
| Turns | 3 |
| Track width | 0.50 mm |
| Gap | 0.50 mm |
| Resulting inner opening | about 31.0 x 23.0 mm |
| Layer | F.Cu only |

Keep all copper on every other layer at least 1.5 mm away from the antenna
conductor. Do not route planes, switch-node copper, USB, SPI, or RF feed lines
under the loop. Keep the matching network within 5 mm of the loop terminals
and use symmetrical, equal-length connections. Components may occupy the
central opening only after their detuning effect has been included in the
assembled-board measurement.

Before release, measure loop inductance and Q with the final stackup and
enclosure, tune the complete differential network at 13.56 MHz with a VNA or
NFC tuning fixture, then verify field strength, load modulation, detuning by a
card/phone, and PN532 TVDD current below its 150 mA maximum.

power, antenna, and duty cycle must be configured for the legal regional band;
the 868 MHz build is not a universal-frequency transmitter.

100 mA startup current. Its controlled rise time must keep the V_IO ramp within
the u-blox limit of 25 us/V to 35,000 us/V; targeting roughly 0.2 ms to 5 ms at
3.3 V gives useful margin. When GNSS V_IO is off, the ESP must not drive RXD,
TIMEPULSE, or any other module PIO; the optional 1 kOhm UART resistors limit
fault/back-power current during firmware mistakes.

### RF route and active-antenna option

The default, lowest-risk build is a passive external GNSS antenna:

`U.FL -> optional low-capacitance ESD (<0.3 pF) -> optional SAW footprint ->
47 pF C0G DC-coupling footprint -> RF_IN`.

RF_IN already contains a DC block. The extra 47 pF position is retained so the
same layout can implement u-blox's antenna supervisor and be reworked during
bring-up. Populate/DNP based on the final reference topology; do not cascade a
filter without measuring its insertion loss.

For an active two-wire antenna, copy the u-blox integration-manual supervisor
schematic as a unit, not individual fragments:

- L3: 27 nH, 5%, high-Q, self-resonant impedance greater than 500 Ohm in the
  GNSS band, current rating at least 300 mA.
- C14: 10 nF, X7R, 16 V; C18: 47 pF, C0G, 25 V.
- R7: 100 kOhm; R8: 10 Ohm, at least 0.25 W.
- T1/T2: Si1016X dual MOSFET or pin/function-equivalent validated substitute.
- U7: NC7WZ07P6X open-drain buffer or validated equivalent.
- `LNA_EN` controls antenna power; the short-detect open-drain output connects
  to EXTINT. Use the matching UBX antenna-supervisor firmware configuration.

Put U.FL, ESD, optional SAW, bias inductor, and RF_IN in a straight, very short
chain. Route as a 50 Ohm grounded coplanar waveguide calculated from the **actual
JLCPCB four-layer stackup**; use a solid L2 GND plane and via fence. No vias or
stubs in the RF trace if avoidable. Keep at least 5 mm from DC/DC inductors,
ESP32/module antennas, SD clock, NFC transmitter, and digital edge traces.
Maintain solid GND under the MAX-M10S and do not route signals below it.

## J_SD microSD (Molex 104031-0811)

Official references:

- [Molex 104031 series](https://www.molex.com/en-us/products/series-chart/104031)
- [SD Association simplified specifications](https://www.sdcard.org/downloads/pls/)

The selected connector is the Molex 104031-0811 push-pull, 1.42 mm-high socket
with card-detect switch. It occupies about 11.91 x 11.4 mm; the KiCad official
footprint courtyard is approximately 13.68 x 13.05 mm. Preserve a clear card
insertion/removal corridor to the board edge.

| microSD pin | Native name | SPI-mode connection |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | DAT2 | 47 kOhm pull-up to +3V3, otherwise unused |
| 2 | CD/DAT3 | `SD_CS_N` / GPIO17, 47 kOhm pull-up, optional 33 Ohm series |
| 3 | CMD | `SPI_MOSI`, 47 kOhm pull-up, 33 Ohm branch damping |
| 4 | VDD | `+3V3`; 100 nF + 10 uF + 47 uF low-ESR near socket |
| 5 | CLK | `SPI_SCK`, 33 Ohm damping at source/branch |
| 6 | VSS | GND |
| 7 | DAT0 | `SPI_MISO`, 47 kOhm pull-up, optional 33 Ohm near card |
| 8 | DAT1 | 47 kOhm pull-up, otherwise unused |
| 9/10 | detect switch | One side GND, other `SD_DETECT_N` + 10 kOhm pull-up |
| shell | shield | GND with multiple short vias |

The detect switch is isolated and its two contacts are electrically
interchangeable, but pad numbering still has to match the Molex drawing and
official KiCad footprint. Fit a low-capacitance ESD array on the touched signal
contacts if routing permits. Keep the SD branch short and star-connected; do
not daisy-chain through the E07 module.

## Infrared transmit and receive

Official references:

- [TSOP753..W receiver data sheet](https://www.vishay.com/docs/82497/tsop753w.pdf)
- [TSAL6200 emitter data sheet](https://www.vishay.com/docs/81010/tsal6200.pdf)

### U13 TSOP75338WTR receiver

| Pin | Function | Connection |
|---:|---|---|
| 1, 4 | GND | GND |
| 2 | VS | `+3V3` through 100 Ohm; 100 nF + 4.7 uF at receiver |
| 3 | OUT | `IR_RX` / GPIO37; optional DNP 10 kOhm pull-up to +3V3 |

The official W package is 6.8 mm wide, 3.0 mm high, and 2.35 mm deep. Put its
optical face at the PCB edge. Keep the +/-75 degree field of view clear and
shield it optically from D_IR with enclosure plastic, an opaque divider, or
placement on a different edge. Supply filtering and physical separation from
the boost converter are more important than a short GPIO trace.

### D_IR TSAL6200 transmitter

Use the 5 mm THT emitter at the edge so the difficult SMT parts remain factory
assembled and the user can hand-fit/bend the LED after bring-up:

`+5V_AUX -> 39 Ohm / 1 W (2512) -> TSAL6200 anode; cathode -> AO3400A drain;
AO3400A source -> GND; gate <- GPIO36 through 100 Ohm; 100 kOhm gate pulldown`.

The 39 Ohm default limits the worst-case current to about 100 mA at a high 5 V
rail and low LED forward voltage. This is already a strong narrow-beam remote
signal; Vishay specifies 16 m typical receiver range with only 50 mA in its
TSOP test circuit. A lower resistor is a controlled prototype option only
after checking the emitter pulse-current derating graph, actual protocol burst
length, resistor pulse rating, and optical safety.

One TSAL6200 must **never** be driven at the old 800 mA project-budget value.
Its 200 mA rating applies only to short, low-duty pulses under the data-sheet
condition, not arbitrary IR protocol bursts. Firmware must keep the 5 V boost
off by default, enforce burst/envelope duty limits, prevent a stuck-on output,
and block simultaneous unrestricted 5 V header load. Perform an IEC 62471
optical safety review and do not view the emitter directly at close range.

## U12 PCF8563 RTC

Official reference: [NXP PCF8563 data sheet](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/data-sheet/PCF8563.pdf)

| Pin | Name | Connection |
|---:|---|---|
| 1, 2 | OSCI, OSCO | 32.768 kHz crystal, CL = 8 pF target, Rs <= 100 kOhm |
| 3 | INT | NC/test pad; open-drain if later used |
| 4 | VSS | GND |
| 5, 6 | SDA, SCL | Shared I2C |
| 7 | CLKOUT | Test pad; open-drain, disabled by firmware when unused |
| 8 | VDD | `+3V3` or an explicitly defined always-on 3.3 V rail; 100 nF |

The 7-bit address is 0x51. Use a non-polar 3215 two-pad crystal with CL = 8 pF
and preferably Rs below 70 kOhm. No two external load capacitors are required
because PCF8563 integrates its oscillator capacitor; retain only the data-sheet
Ctrim position (5 pF to 25 pF on OSCI) as DNP if calibration is planned. Keep
crystal traces extremely short, symmetric, and free of test pads; no fast
traces or copper pours directly below them. CLKOUT starts enabled at 32.768 kHz,
so firmware should disable it early when unused. If the RTC remains powered
while the ESP/I2C rail is off, pull SDA/SCL to the same always-on domain or add
isolation so the bus cannot back-power other devices.

## New package footprints and exact geometry

Two previously unresolved packages are supplied as a separate project-local
library, without changing the active board or library table:

- `hardware/PocketLab_Feature.pretty/BMP390_LGA10_2x2mm.kicad_mod`
- `hardware/PocketLab_Feature.pretty/TSOP75338WTR_HeimdallW_SideView.kicad_mod`

BMP390 footprint geometry follows the Bosch bottom view/landing-pattern rule:

- Body 2.00 x 2.00 mm, typical height 0.75 mm.
- Side pads (3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10): 0.275 x 0.250 mm at x = +/-0.7625 mm and
  y = -0.50, 0, +0.50 mm.
- Top/bottom pads (1, 2, 6, 7): 0.250 x 0.275 mm at x = +/-0.25 mm and
  y = +/-0.7625 mm.
- Coordinates in the supplied manufacturer-top-view orientation are:
  P1 `(0.25,-0.7625)`, P2 `(-0.25,-0.7625)`, P3..P5 on x `-0.7625`,
  P6 `(-0.25,0.7625)`, P7 `(0.25,0.7625)`, and P8..P10 on x `+0.7625`.
  Pin 1 is therefore in the top-right quadrant exactly as in the Bosch top
  view; rotate the complete footprint on the PCB rather than mirroring it.
- The central pressure port is marked on fabrication/user layers. No via or
  copper is allowed under the body except the ten land pads.

TSOP75338WTR footprint geometry follows Vishay's proposed component-side pad
layout:

- Body 6.8 x 2.35 mm, height 3.0 mm.
- Four pads 0.8 x 1.8 mm on 1.27 mm pitch (3 x 1.27 = 3.81 mm span).
- In the supplied footprint, body center is `(0,0)`, the optical face is the
  long edge at y `-1.175`, and rear lead-land centers are at y `+1.55` with
  x = `-1.905, -0.635, +0.635, +1.905` for pins 1 through 4.
- Optical-face direction, pin 1, and the edge-facing installation direction
  are explicitly marked.

The E07 custom footprint already present in `PocketLab_Custom.pretty` uses the
official 20 x 14 mm body, 1.27 mm pitch, and 0.8 x 1.8 mm castellated lands.
MAX-M10S uses the u-blox 18-LCC land pattern (body 9.7 x 10.1 mm, 2.5 mm max
height); use the official u-blox/KiCad footprint and the integration-manual
stencil apertures rather than redrawing it.

## Fabrication-release blockers

The following items must be closed before Gerber/BOM/CPL export:

1. Capture these nets and safe-state resistors in the real hierarchical
   schematic, annotate, and update PCB from schematic.
2. Import the two new footprint files only after independent drawing review;
   verify pin-1 orientation against the JLC/LCSC component preview.
3. Calculate 50 Ohm GNSS geometry from JLC's ordered four-layer stackup and
   validate the chosen U.FL, ESD, and optional SAW footprints.
4. Measure and tune the NFC loop on the first assembled panel; do not release
   volume boards with guessed C1/C2/RQ values.
5. Correct every reference to an 800 mA single-LED IR mode; the safe default is
   approximately 100 mA with 39 Ohm / 1 W.
6. Confirm microSD connector availability and exact 104031-0811 pad/detect
   orientation before BOM lock.
7. Run KiCad ERC and DRC with zero unexplained errors, then perform a schematic,
   footprint, polarity, antenna-clearance, and assembly-side peer review.
