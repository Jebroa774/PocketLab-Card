# Detailed feature design baseline

This document is the schematic and layout baseline for the non-power feature
blocks. It is deliberately more specific than the architecture notes, but it
is **not** a fabrication release: NFC tuning, controlled-impedance geometry,
antenna selection, ERC/DRC, and a first-article review remain mandatory.

The current dense layout plan uses two-sided JLCPCB PCBA. Parts with hidden
pads, LGA, fine-pitch FPC, and the microSD connector are factory-only. General passives
remain 0805; 0603 is used only where RF performance or package geometry
requires it.

## Board-wide interface rules

- `+3V3` is the logic rail. No ESP32, TCA9535, sensor, SD, or radio I/O is
  5 V tolerant.
- Fit one shared I2C pull-up pair only. Use 3.3 kOhm to `+3V3` as the default
  because the PN532 reference design uses 3.3 kOhm at 3.3 V. A 4.7 kOhm
  alternative is allowed only if measured SDA/SCL rise time remains below
  300 ns at 400 kHz with the assembled board and expansion header unloaded.
- The shared SPI bus is limited to 8 MHz in the first firmware because the E07
  module is specified to 10 MHz and the microSD branch increases bus loading.
  The captured Sub-GHz and microSD branches use 22-ohm damping resistors.
- Every J5 digital net has a populated series stage: 100 ohm on the 11 direct
  GPIOs, the GPIO9 board-temperature probe and exposed I2C/SPI buses; 220 ohm
  on EX0-EX7. U24/U25 add shunt ESD
  protection to the five shared I2C/SPI header nets. The remaining direct and
  expander GPIO resistors do not replace a connector-side protection device if
  the deployment requires full-port ESD or overvoltage tolerance.
- All RF values below are initial values, not promises of a tuned antenna.
  C0G/NP0 capacitors and high-Q RF inductors are required.

GPIO9/ADC1_CH8 is reserved for R736/RT701, a 10-kohm/10-kohm board-temperature
divider located beside the 5-V converter area. J5 pin 7 reaches the same node
through R714 as a measurement point only; external circuitry must remain
high-impedance and must never drive this pin.

## U2 PN532 NFC controller

The PN532 is retained because it matches the requested feature set and has a
known software ecosystem. NXP marks it **not recommended for new designs** and
recommends PN7160 for new products. Keep the NFC sheet sufficiently isolated
that a future controller revision is possible.

The PN532 I2C address is 0x24 in 7-bit notation (0x48 as the write address byte
used in older NXP documentation).

Official references:

- [PN532/C1 data sheet](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/nxp/data-sheets/PN532_C1.pdf)
- [PN532 C106 application note](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/nxp/application-notes/153710.pdf)
- [NXP NFC antenna design hub](https://www.nxp.com/products/rfid-nfc/nfc-hf/nfc-readers/nfc-antenna-design-hub%3ANFC-ANTENNA-DESIGN-TOOL)

`DVDD` is generated by the PN532 internal LDO. In the captured schematic pins
5/TVDD, 8/AVDD and 39/DVDD are directly on `NFC_DVDD`, with separate local
decoupling: C313/C304 at DVDD, C314/C315 at TVDD and C302 at AVDD. DVDD must
never be tied to the external +3V3 input. The two 560 nH inductors are the
TX1/TX2 EMC filter inductors, **not** supply inductors.

The implemented receive branch follows the NXP application diagram topology:
`NFC_LOOP_A -> R305 2.7 kOhm -> NFC_RX_AC -> C318 1 nF -> RX`, while R306
1 kOhm biases RX from VMID. C301 100 nF decouples VMID to GND. These parts must stay
close to U2 and the matching network.

### Antenna and matching implementation

Use the symmetrical NXP starting topology on both TX paths:

- `TX1/TX2 -> L0 -> matching network -> loop`, with `L0 = 560 nH` and
  `C0 = 220 pF` on each side as the initial EMC-filter values.
- R303/R304 are removable 0-ohm links between the EMC network and the loop.
  C310 (differential) and C311/C312 (each side to GND) are DNP C0G tuning
  positions. Their eventual values must be derived from measurements, not from
  the placeholder schematic.
- AE1 pads 1/2 and the removable links provide access for bare-loop and network
  measurements during prototype tuning.

PCB-loop prototype geometry inside the reserved NFC area:

| Parameter | Initial value |
|---|---:|
| Copper envelope | 35.0 x 27.0 mm |
| Full foreign-copper/footprint keep-out | 36.0 x 29.0 mm |
| Turns | 4 |
| Track width | 0.50 mm |
| Gap | 0.50 mm |
| Resulting inner opening | about 28.0 x 20.0 mm |
| Main winding | F.Cu, with one short B.Cu crossover through plated pads |

The project-local `PocketLab_Custom:NFC_Loop_35x27mm_4T_TUNE` footprint embeds
the 36 x 29 mm keep-out for foreign tracks, vias, pads, pours and footprints on
F.Cu, B.Cu, In1.Cu and In2.Cu, with only small relief for its own terminals and
crossover. Do not route planes, switch-node copper, USB, SPI or another RF path
through it. Keep the matching network immediately beside the loop terminals
and use symmetrical, equal-length connections.

Before release, measure loop inductance and Q with the final stackup and
enclosure, tune the complete differential network at 13.56 MHz with a VNA or
NFC tuning fixture, then verify field strength, load modulation, detuning by a
card/phone, and PN532 TVDD current below its 150 mA maximum.

## U3 E07-900MM10S Sub-GHz module

Official references:

- [E07-900MM10S product page](https://www.ebyte.com/product/1959.html)

The module is 10 x 10 mm, uses 1.27 mm castellated-pad pitch, operates from
2.5 V to 3.6 V, and contains the CC1101 crystal and internal RF network.

| Pin(s) | Function | Connection |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | VCC | `+3V3`; local 4.7 uF + 100 nF |
| 2, 7, 16 | GND | GND with short stitching vias |
| 3-5, 8-11, 17, 19 | NC | Leave unconnected as specified |
| 6 | ANT | `SUBGHZ_RF_MOD` into C403/R405/C404 and A1 |
| 12 | MISO/GDO1 | `SUB_MISO`, then R403 22 ohm to `SPI_MISO` |
| 13 | MOSI | `SPI_MOSI` through R402 22 ohm |
| 14 | CSN | `SUB_CS_N`; R404 22 ohm and R406 100-kohm pull-up |
| 15 | SCK | `SPI_SCK` through R401 22 ohm |
| 18 | GDO0 | `SUBGHZ_GDO0` / GPIO15 |
| 20 | GDO2 | `SUBGHZ_GDO2` / GPIO16 |

Pin 6 reaches the antenna through a tuneable pi network: C403 and C404 are DNP
shunt sites and R405 is the initial 0-ohm series element. A1 is a hand-soldered
T3-868M spring. Its 17.3-mm body lies in an 18.8 x 6.58 mm open-bottom pocket;
a 0.6-mm PCB bridge provides the required copper-to-edge clearance before the
adjacent pad. That right-hand pad is the only electrical connection. After
soldering and inspection, bond the free left end to the upper FR4 pocket wall
with a small amount of nonconductive epoxy or neutral-cure silicone. This gives
two-point mechanical support without shorting the monopole or deliberately
adding a capacitive second copper pad. Perform final S11 tuning after the bond
has cured and with the enclosure and battery fitted.

Keep a solid GND reference below the module and up to the controlled RF launch,
then keep copper, vias, fast digital routes, switch nodes, the battery and
enclosure metal out of the spring volume. The external match is a prototype
starting point: measure S11 with the final PCB stack, enclosure and battery,
then populate C403/R405/C404 from measured evidence.
Frequency, power, antenna, and duty cycle must be configured for the legal regional band;
the 868 MHz build is not a universal-frequency transmitter, and successful
prototype operation is not proof of regulatory compliance.

## U4 HTRC110 125 kHz LF-RFID and secure pairing

Official references:

- [NXP HTRC110 data sheet](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/data-sheet/037031.pdf)
- [NXP AN98080 HTRC110 application note](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/application-note/AN98080.pdf)
- [Microchip ATECC608C product page](https://www.microchip.com/en-us/product/atecc608c)

U4 is powered from switched `LF_5V`; U17/TPS22919 is enabled by U18 P4 only
after the 5-V boost is stable. Y501 supplies the permitted 4-MHz CMOS clock.
GPIO18 SCLK and GPIO35 DIN pass through U21/SN74AHCT125 for valid 5-V logic;
U4 DOUT returns through U22/SN74LVC1G17 to GPIO21. MODE is grounded for the
normal short local serial interface.

### Resonant path

J3 is a 2.54-mm two-pin connector for a removable nominal 400-uH coil. C505
provides coil-path isolation, R502 is a 47-ohm pulse-rated starting current
limit and C506 is the fitted 3.9-nF C0G starting resonance value. C507/C508 are
100-pF/220-pF C0G DNP trim pads. R503 provides a 1-kohm protected return and
R504 is the 150-kohm RX divider; C509 is an optional DNP RX EMI trim.

Route `LF_TX1`, `LF_TX2`, `LF_ANT_A`, `LF_ANT_B` and `LF_TAP` as one short,
wide, via-free outer-layer loop. Keep `LF_RX`, `LF_QGND` and `LF_CEXT` short
and away from switching nodes. The 3.9-nF/400-uH combination is not an order
value: measure coil inductance and Q, phase, peak current, tap voltage and
HTRC110 `ANTFAIL` on the assembled prototype before final capacitor selection.

### Secure element and PAIR button

U23 is the hand-reworkable SOIC-8 ATECC608C-SSHDA-T on the shared I2C bus.
SW6 pulls `PAIR_N`/GPIO38 low. Development boards ship with the secure element
unprovisioned; locking its zones and enabling ESP32 secure-boot/flash-encryption
eFuses are irreversible production steps requiring a reviewed key manifest,
recovery procedure and app protocol. Firmware may open a short pairing window
only while SW6 is physically held, but this alone is not persistent ownership.

## J2 microSD (Molex 104031-0811)

Official references:

- [Molex 104031 series](https://www.molex.com/en-us/products/series-chart/104031)
- [SD Association simplified specifications](https://www.sdcard.org/downloads/pls/)

The selected connector is the Molex 104031-0811 push-pull, 1.42 mm-high socket
with card-detect switch. It occupies about 11.91 x 11.4 mm; the KiCad official
footprint courtyard is approximately 13.68 x 13.05 mm. Preserve a clear card
insertion/removal corridor to the board edge.

| microSD pin | Native name | SPI-mode connection |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | DAT2 | 47 kOhm pull-up to +3V3, otherwise unused |
| 2 | CD/DAT3 | `SD_CS_N` / GPIO17 through R510 22 ohm; R515 47-kohm pull-up |
| 3 | CMD | `SPI_MOSI` through R511 22 ohm; R516 47-kohm pull-up |
| 4 | VDD | `+3V3`; 100 nF + 10 uF + 47 uF low-ESR near socket |
| 5 | CLK | `SPI_SCK` through R512 22 ohm |
| 6 | VSS | GND |
| 7 | DAT0 | `SPI_MISO` through R513 22 ohm; R517 47-kohm pull-up |
| 8 | DAT1 | 47 kOhm pull-up, otherwise unused |
| 9/10 | detect switch | One side GND, other `SD_DETECT_N` + 10 kOhm pull-up |
| shell | shield | GND with multiple short vias |

The detect switch is isolated and its two contacts are electrically
interchangeable, but pad numbering still has to match the Molex drawing and
official KiCad footprint. U19 (`SRV05-4MR6T1G`) clamps CS, MOSI, SCK and MISO;
place it at the socket side of R510-R513. C510 100 nF, C511 10 uF and C512
47 uF/6.3 V low-ESR provide local write-transient decoupling. Keep the SD
branch short and star-connected; do not daisy-chain through the E07 module.

## Infrared transmit and receive

Official references:

- [TSOP753..W receiver data sheet](https://www.vishay.com/docs/82497/tsop753w.pdf)
- [TSAL6200 emitter data sheet](https://www.vishay.com/docs/81010/tsal6200.pdf)

### U13 TSOP75338WTR receiver

| Pin | Function | Connection |
|---:|---|---|
| 1, 4 | GND | GND |
| 2 | VS | `+3V3` through 100 Ohm; 100 nF + 4.7 uF at receiver |
| 3 | OUT | `IR_RX` / GPIO37; optional DNP 10 kOhm pull-up to +3V3 |

The official W package is 6.8 mm wide, 3.0 mm high, and 2.35 mm deep. Put its
optical face at the PCB edge. Keep the +/-75 degree field of view clear and
shield it optically from D1 with enclosure plastic, an opaque divider, or
placement on a different edge. Supply filtering and physical separation from
the boost converter are more important than a short GPIO trace.

### D1 TSAL6200 transmitter

Use the 5 mm THT emitter at the edge so the difficult SMT parts remain factory
assembled and the user can hand-fit/bend the LED after bring-up:

`+5V_RAW -> 39 Ohm / 1 W (2512) -> TSAL6200 anode; cathode -> AO3400A drain;
AO3400A source -> GND; gate <- GPIO36 through 100 Ohm; 100 kOhm gate pulldown`.

The 39 Ohm default limits the worst-case current to about 100 mA at a high 5 V
rail and low LED forward voltage. This is already a strong narrow-beam remote
signal; Vishay specifies 16 m typical receiver range with only 50 mA in its
TSOP test circuit. A lower resistor is a controlled prototype option only
after checking the emitter pulse-current derating graph, actual protocol burst
length, resistor pulse rating, and optical safety.

One TSAL6200 must **never** be driven at the old 800 mA project-budget value.
Its 200 mA rating applies only to short, low-duty pulses under the data-sheet
condition, not arbitrary IR protocol bursts. Firmware must keep the 5 V boost
off by default, enforce burst/envelope duty limits, prevent a stuck-on output,
and block simultaneous unrestricted 5 V header load. Perform an IEC 62471
optical safety review and do not view the emitter directly at close range.

## Compact OLED

J8 is a bare EastRising `ER-OLED0.42-1W` white OLED with an SSD1306B,
72 x 40 pixels and a 12 x 11 x 1.25 mm glass envelope. It uses I2C address
`0x3C` on the shared 3.3-V bus. The 16-way solder FPC has 0.65-mm pitch; it is
soldered to the PCB first and the glass is then folded and bonded over the land
pattern. It is not a removable daughterboard.

C610/C611 are the 1-uF charge-pump flying capacitors. C612/C613 decouple VDD
and C614-C616 support VCOMH/VCC. All seven parts are 0805 for hand rework.
`ESP_EN` resets the display at power-up, while BS1, CS and D/C strap the panel
for I2C. Inspect FPC pin 1, exposed-contact side and every solder joint before
folding because a reversed panel cannot be corrected in firmware.

SW3, SW7 and SW4 are the adjacent `UP`, `OK` and `DOWN` navigation controls.
All three use the same C&K KMR221GLFS footprint as the RESET/BOOT service
buttons. R608, R607 and R609 provide the respective 10-kohm pull-ups. SELECT
uses U9/P07; it does not share the dedicated PAIR security input.

## U9/U18 I/O expanders

Official references: [TI TCA9535 data sheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tca9535.pdf)
and [TI TCA9534 data sheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tca9534.pdf).

The design separates monitoring/expansion from internal control. Both devices
share `IOEXP_INT_N` and the I2C bus. R703 is the 10-kohm interrupt pull-up, and
R701/R702 are the only board-wide 3.3-kohm SDA/SCL pull-ups. Neither expander
has internal GPIO pull resistors; all ports power up as inputs.

U9 is a TCA9535PWR at 7-bit address `0x20` (A0/A1/A2 low):

| Port | Connection |
|---|---|
| P00 | `SD_DETECT_N` |
| P01 | `CHARGER_CHG_N` |
| P02 | `CHARGER_PGOOD_N` |
| P03 | `AUX5_FAULT_N` |
| P04 | `FG_ALERT_N` |
| P05 | `BMI_INT1` |
| P06 | `BMI_INT2` |
| P07 | `USER_BUTTON_SELECT_N`, active-low OK/SELECT input |
| P10-P17 | `EX0_INT`-`EX7_INT`; R722-R729 220 ohm to J5 EX0-EX7 |

U18 is a TCA9534PWR at 7-bit address `0x21` (A0 high, A1/A2 low):

| Port | Connection / role |
|---|---|
| P0 | `BQ_EN1`, charger input-mode control |
| P1 | `CHG_DISABLE`, active-high charge disable |
| P2 | `AUX5_EN`, protected header-output enable |
| P3 | `NFC_RESET_N` |
| P4 | `LF_RFID_EN` |
| P5 | `BOOST5_EN` |
| P6 | `USER_BUTTON_A_N`, active-low UP input |
| P7 | `USER_BUTTON_B_N`, active-low DOWN input |

External pull resistors hold charger, LF RFID, NFC and 5-V controls in their safe
states until firmware has discovered and configured U18. Firmware must never
assume the expanders retained their direction/output registers across a reset.

## U12 PCF8563 RTC

Official reference: [NXP PCF8563 data sheet](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/data-sheet/PCF8563.pdf)

| Pin | Name | Connection |
|---:|---|---|
| 1, 2 | OSCI, OSCO | Y701 32.768 kHz, CL = 7 pF, Rs <= 100 kOhm |
| 3 | INT | NC/test pad; open-drain if later used |
| 4 | VSS | GND |
| 5, 6 | SDA, SCL | Shared I2C |
| 7 | CLKOUT | Test pad; open-drain, disabled by firmware when unused |
| 8 | VDD | `+3V3` or an explicitly defined always-on 3.3 V rail; 100 nF |

The 7-bit address is 0x51. Y701 is a non-polar 3215 two-pad crystal with CL = 7 pF
and preferably Rs below 70 kOhm. No two external load capacitors are required
because PCF8563 integrates its oscillator capacitor; retain only the data-sheet
Ctrim position (5 pF to 25 pF on OSCI) as DNP if calibration is planned. Keep
crystal traces extremely short, symmetric, and free of test pads; no fast
traces or copper pours directly below them. CLKOUT starts enabled at 32.768 kHz,
so firmware should disable it early when unused. If the RTC remains powered
while the ESP/I2C rail is off, pull SDA/SCL to the same always-on domain or add
isolation so the bus cannot back-power other devices.

## Project-local package footprints and exact geometry

All non-stock packages used by the schematic are in
`hardware/PocketLab_Custom.pretty` and are registered by `fp-lib-table`.
This includes BMP390, TSOP75338WTR, TPS63070, both power inductors, the E07
module and the NFC loop. These land patterns still require independent drawing,
pin-one and assembler-preview review before release.

BMP390 footprint geometry follows the Bosch bottom view/landing-pattern rule:

- Body 2.00 x 2.00 mm, typical height 0.75 mm.
- Side pads (3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10): 0.275 x 0.250 mm at x = +/-0.7625 mm and
  y = -0.50, 0, +0.50 mm.
- Top/bottom pads (1, 2, 6, 7): 0.250 x 0.275 mm at x = +/-0.25 mm and
  y = +/-0.7625 mm.
- Coordinates in the supplied manufacturer-top-view orientation are:
  P1 `(0.25,-0.7625)`, P2 `(-0.25,-0.7625)`, P3..P5 on x `-0.7625`,
  P6 `(-0.25,0.7625)`, P7 `(0.25,0.7625)`, and P8..P10 on x `+0.7625`.
  Pin 1 is therefore in the top-right quadrant exactly as in the Bosch top
  view; rotate the complete footprint on the PCB rather than mirroring it.
- The central pressure port is marked on fabrication/user layers. No via or
  copper is allowed under the body except the ten land pads.

TSOP75338WTR footprint geometry follows Vishay's proposed component-side pad
layout:

- Body 6.8 x 2.35 mm, height 3.0 mm.
- Four pads 0.8 x 1.8 mm on 1.27 mm pitch (3 x 1.27 = 3.81 mm span).
- In the supplied footprint, body center is `(0,0)`, the optical face is the
  long edge at y `-1.175`, and rear lead-land centers are at y `+1.55` with
  x = `-1.905, -0.635, +0.635, +1.905` for pins 1 through 4.
- Optical-face direction, pin 1, and the edge-facing installation direction
  are explicitly marked.

The E07 custom footprint already present in `PocketLab_Custom.pretty` uses the
official 20 x 14 mm body, 1.27 mm pitch, and 0.8 x 1.8 mm castellated lands.
HTRC110 and ATECC608C deliberately use standard SOIC packages so both remain
inspectable and hand-reworkable after factory assembly.

AE1 uses `NFC_Loop_35x27mm_4T_TUNE`: 35 x 27 mm copper, four turns,
0.50/0.50 mm track/spacing, and a short plated B.Cu crossover. The footprint is
excluded from BOM/CPL and embeds the 36 x 29 mm all-layer keep-out. Its roughly
1.0-1.4 uH first-order estimate is only a placement aid, never a measured value.

## Prototype and release blockers

Before exporting an engineering-prototype Gerber/BOM/CPL set:

1. Independently review all project-local and stock footprints, pin-one
   orientation, polarity and JLC/LCSC component previews.
2. Finish physical placement and routing, preserving ESP32, NFC, LF RFID and
   Sub-GHz keep-outs and return paths; close DRC with no unexplained items.
3. Calculate 90-ohm USB and 50-ohm Sub-GHz geometry from the actually ordered
   stack-up; validate LF coil resonance, current, voltage and DNP tune sites.
4. Confirm microSD connector and U19 availability plus exact 104031-0811
   pad/detect orientation before BOM lock.
5. Verify the approximately 100-mA IR design and fitted 39-ohm/1-W part, enforce
   firmware duty limits and complete the optical-safety review.
6. Repeat ERC/DRC and perform schematic, footprint, polarity,
   antenna-clearance, both-side assembly and manufacturing-output peer review.

The first cards remain engineering prototypes. Before any volume release,
measure loop inductance/Q and tune the complete NFC match on the assembled card
with a VNA or NFC fixture. Also verify LF resonance/range, Sub-GHz radiated
behavior, power/thermal margins and applicable RF/regulatory limits using the
actual antennas, enclosure and battery.
